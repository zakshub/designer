# Human review and quality bar

Publication requires review of attribution, rights, scope, observation versus interpretation, source independence, conflicts and confidence rationale. The local CLI records the reviewer's attestation; it does not authenticate identity. Fixture reviews are explicitly simulated.

Contributions explain what was observed, under which constraints, how it was evaluated, what might contradict it and how it could change a decision. Generic aesthetic adjectives alone are insufficient.

Rejection records a reason. Content changes clear approval and invalidate dependents. Outcome-driven changes require renewed review. Review dates are mandatory; stale, retired and superseded objects remain auditable but cannot guide active retrieval.

The software enforces structure and transitions. Human reviewers judge substantive design quality, originality, attribution correctness and experimental validity.
