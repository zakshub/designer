# Evidence policy

Claims retain original sources, supporting/counterevidence, applicability, limitations, dates, reviews and confidence rationale. Evidence separates observation from interpretation and records method and location.

Sources identify creator, publisher, origin group, rights and strength. Unknown, prohibited or metadata-only rights cannot publish for active use; they can remain draft leads.

Weak sources alone cannot publish guidance. A reviewed non-weak source can support provisional knowledge. Canonical claims and high confidence require two independent non-weak origins. Repeated reporting from one origin counts once. No source count makes a claim universal.

Conflicts stay explicit. Dates and upstream changes invalidate guidance. Retirement preserves audit history. Ingestion is manual and begins in draft; external material is not fetched or published autonomously.

Supplied examples are fictional test records and must never be cited as actual design research.
