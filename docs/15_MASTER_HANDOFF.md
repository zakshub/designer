## Designer and Studio relationship

Designer Brain owns design intelligence.

Studio Brain owns imagery production intelligence.

Designer creates image requirements.

Studio produces image reasoning and imagery.

Studio performs image QC.

Designer performs design fit QC.

Neither brain hallucinates the specialist expertise of the other.
