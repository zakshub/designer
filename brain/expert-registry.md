# Expert registry

Use expert-profile objects with source IDs, domains, expertise basis, philosophy and limits. Preserve conflicting viewpoints as distinct attributable positions.

The original supplied fixture profile remains fictional and must not be cited as actual research.

## Curated real-source working registry

For brand, category, color and visual-language reasoning, use:

- [Brand Intelligence — Experts, Research and Reference Sources](brand-intelligence/experts-and-sources.md)

That registry includes attributed academic researchers, practitioner SMEs/studios, platform/design-system authorities, critical publications and shipped-product reference libraries. Inclusion is not endorsement and does not make a source canonical.

Real expert claims must retain source provenance and limitations. Co-authors from one paper count as one evidence origin when evaluating independence. Practitioner case studies are mechanism/intent evidence, not causal proof. Editorial criticism remains attributed opinion.
