# Supervised ingestion

1. Discover a source for a declared question; capture attribution, rights, origin group and date.
2. Extract a bounded observation, method, locator, scope and limitations; separate interpretation.
3. Ingest drafts and validate their schemas/links.
4. Submit/review sources before evidence, and evidence before claims.
5. Retrieve by context; record the used published revision and actual produced artifact.
6. Record critique, outcome evidence and a project-learning record.
7. Review the outcome, apply it once, then review updated guidance.
8. Retain, correct, supersede or retire.

The backend records these activities and enforces transitions. Discovery, interpretation, production and substantive critique are human/external activities.
